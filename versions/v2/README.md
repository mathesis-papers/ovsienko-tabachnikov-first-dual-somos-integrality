# Ovsienko–Tabachnikov's dual Somos integrality conjectures: a two-weight extension of Hone's divisibility proof

**Authors:** Haobo Ma, Wenlin Zhang

Extending Hone's divisibility proof (2007, Proposition 1) and Ovsienko and Tabachnikov's sufficient conditions, we settle the conjecture immediately after Corollary 5.2 in Section 5.4, printed pages 10–11 of arXiv:1705.01623v1 (2017), by classifying integer shadows of both dual Somos deformations. For positive integers p,q, integer weights u,v, and each fixed integer initial shadow tuple, the recurrence A_(n+4) A_n = (1+uε)(A_(n+1) A_(n+3))^p + (1+vε) A_(n+2)^q has integer shadows at every index exactly when (q−2)u = (2p−2)v. In the first conjectured family, u=0,v=1, this is equivalent to p=1; if p≥2, the first noninteger shadow is always at index 9. In the second family, u=1,v=0, the criterion is q=2. More generally, the first failure and its reduced denominator depend only on the real orbit and the integer obstruction, not on the initial shadows. We also give the arbitrary-initial-shadow version of the source's q=0 example and an exact certificate for its reported sign change in the fourth homogeneous shadow.
